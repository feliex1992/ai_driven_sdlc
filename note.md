## Structure .ai

'''
.ai/
│
├── rules/       ← "Bagaimana AI harus bekerja"
├── context/     ← "Apa yang AI perlu tahu"
├── tasks/       ← "Apa yang harus dikerjakan"
├── decisions/   ← "Keputusan arsitektur"
└── reports/     ← "Hasil analysis/test/security/review"
'''

Jadi nanti kita punya separation yang jelas:

Rules = behavior
Context = knowledge
Tasks = work
Decisions = memory
Reports = evidence
